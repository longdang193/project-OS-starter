# Starter Adoption Experience

This workstream exists to improve how projects adopt, understand, and mature
through `project-OS-starter`.

## Jobs To Be Done

- make starter adoption understandable for new projects
- make migration readiness and maturity signals visible
- make the next bounded change under starter adoption easy to choose

## Success Signals

- new repos can identify the right adoption mode and next move quickly
- prompt and guidance surfaces reduce guesswork during adoption and migration
- divergence between starter intent and downstream execution is easier to detect

## Thread Folder

- `docs/intent/workstreams/threads/starter-adoption-experience/`

Belongs here:

- adoption-mode guidance
- migration readiness and maturity signals
- starter usability, prompts, and learning surfaces

Does not belong here:

- repo-only operating-system governance that does not serve downstream adoption
- unrelated product capability work outside starter adoption experience
